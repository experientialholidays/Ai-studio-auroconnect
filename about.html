Auroville: A Living Laboratory

Founded in 1968 based on the spiritual vision of Sri Aurobindo and The Mother, Auroville is a universal township dedicated to human unity, conscious evolution, and sustainable living. It acts as a pioneering living laboratory—an evolving community where people from all walks of life come together to explore new ways of living, learning, creating, working, and growing in progressive harmony.

Whether you're looking for practical information, exploring Auroville for the first time, or simply curious to learn more, AuroConnect is here to help.
