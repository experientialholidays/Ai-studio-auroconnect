About AuroConnect

Your Independent Discovery Platform for Retreats, Workshops & Events

AuroConnect is a community-focused discovery and information platform created to help residents, visitors, guests, and seekers easily find retreats, workshops, volunteer opportunities, cultural programs, and community events in and around Auroville.

Our Purpose

Whether you are seeking personal growth through retreats, looking to share skills in workshops, offering volunteer service, or simply connecting with daily activities, AuroConnect makes discovery simple, transparent, and accessible.

Key Features

  • 🤖 AI-Assisted Discovery: Ask questions naturally to find specific workshops, schedules, venues, and facilitators.
  • 📅 Comprehensive Event Directory: Search and filter through updated listings for retreats, classes, cultural events, and gatherings.
  • ✍️ Easy Event Submissions: Facilitators and organizers can easily submit new events via text forms, Excel files, or PDF posters.
  • 📊 Host & Facilitator Dashboard: Manage submissions, edit listings, and keep event records up to date seamlessly.

Independent Platform Disclaimer: AuroConnect is an independent discovery platform and is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by the Auroville Foundation or any official Auroville unit.
