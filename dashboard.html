          ℹ️ Category Selection Criteria & Rules
        


        

          

            📅 Date-specific Events
            

              One-time or short multi-day events occurring on specific calendar dates (e.g., "15 August 2026" or "20 to 23 August 2026").
            

          


          

            🗓️ Weekly Events
            

              Recurring events taking place on 1 to 5 specific days of the week every week (e.g., "Every Monday & Thursday").
            

          


          

            ☀️ Daily Events
            

              Ongoing daily activities available every day or covering extended continuous periods.
            

          


          

            ⚡ Automatic Category Rules:
            
    
              
  • Date Range > 8 Days: If a date range spans more than 8 days (or if no start date is set), the event automatically becomes a Daily Event.
    • 
              
  • 6 or 7 Weekdays Selected: If 6 or 7 days of the week are selected (or if no specific days are checked), it automatically becomes a Daily Event.
    • 
              
  • 1 to 5 Weekdays Selected: Selecting 1 to 5 days classifies the event as a Weekly Event.
